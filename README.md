#  Telecom Customer Churn Analysis

##  Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing telecom customer data to understand customer churn behavior and identify the factors that influence customers to leave a telecom service.

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) is performed to discover patterns, trends, and insights that can help telecom companies improve customer satisfaction and reduce churn rates.

##  Project Objectives
- Analyze customer data to understand churn patterns.
- Identify the major factors influencing customer churn.
- Understand the relationship between customer tenure, monthly charges, and churn.
- Analyze customer preferences for different telecom services.
- Provide business insights and recommendations to reduce customer churn.

##  Key Insights

- Customers with shorter tenure and higher monthly charges show a greater tendency to churn.
- Gender does not appear to have a significant relationship with customer churn.
- Customers using monthly contracts are more likely to leave compared to customers with longer-term contracts.
- Customers using electronic check payment methods show higher churn.
- Customers without Online Security, Online Backup, Device Protection, and Tech Support services show higher churn.
- Customers without dependents show a higher tendency to leave.
- Streaming TV does not appear to be a major factor influencing churn.

##  Business Recommendations

- Provide special offers to monthly contract customers to encourage yearly subscriptions.
- Improve services and customer experience for Fiber Optic internet users.
- Introduce rewards and improve the payment experience for electronic check users.
- Promote the benefits of Online Security, Online Backup, Device Protection, and Tech Support.
- Provide targeted offers to customers with shorter tenure.
- Improve customer engagement and satisfaction to reduce churn.
